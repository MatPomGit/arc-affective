\begin{abstract}
Time-sensitive multimodal inference must balance output freshness, tail latency, throughput and sensor reliability under finite resources. This manuscript specifies a reproducible edge benchmark that compares FIFO, latest-sample, bounded-queue and a proposed quality- and deadline-aware scheduler with explicit drop accounting. Real-clock measurements, repeatability and physical-node profiling are required to establish its engineering value. The evaluation isolates queueing effects from the predictive accuracy of affect recognition. This is a preregistration-stage draft; no real-node benchmarks, improvements or sensor-inference claims have yet been established.
\end{abstract}
